Prestige notes (skills, carry-over, Labs keycards, hideout)
  • Prestige 0 → 1 is mostly an XP run. Story is optional for it. This route is built from a fresh character for compatibility, but its real target is Prestige 2 and up.
  • Prestige 1 needs no story. PvP needs level 25, Ragman's New Beginning (level 25 to start: 50 Scavs, extract from The Lab, hand in any PMC, Scav, Boss and Trader figurine), the three hideout modules and 10M roubles. PvE needs only level 15 and 5M roubles.
  • This route's chapters map onto Prestige 2–4: Tour for Prestige 2, Falling Skies for 3, and obtaining the Ticket (end of step 13, before Kerman's offer) for 4. Kerman's choice and the endings only matter from Prestige 6.
  • New Beginning (Prestige 2), from Ragman, needs Prestige 1 and level 30 to start (the wiki lists 30 even though PvE Prestige 2 only needs level 25; check in game): eliminate 10 PMCs, extract from The Lab, hand in 2 FiR PMC figurines, 2 FiR Scav figurines, 2 FiR Boss figurines and 2 FiR Trader figurines.
  • Skills level from normal play. Strength levels while you move over your weight limit (the weight number turns from grey to yellow); Endurance levels while you move under it (grey number); the hideout Gym (Defective Wall 6, Illumination 2, Vents 2) also trains both. You don't need your own: open the player profile of a friend who has a Gym in the same game mode (PvP or PvE), choose view hideout, press Enter to walk in, go to their Gym and use the weight bench. Charisma comes from spending money with traders on items and services. Each Prestige also grants +1 Charisma.
    • Skill fatigue: in a raid, each skill slows sharply after about 5 points (red arrow next to the skill; 1.3.0.0 retuned this, so trust the arrow), and recovers to full speed after about 200 seconds (3⅓ minutes) without gains. So alternate: about 3½ minutes overweight (Strength), then about 3½ minutes under the limit (Endurance), and repeat for the whole raid.
    • Solo: 3-(b-TG) stim loop. Pack so you're just over the limit. The 3-(b-TG) gives Strength +10 for 4 minutes, which lifts your carry limit: stimmed you're under it (Endurance), and when it wears off you're over (Strength). One stim covers an 8-minute cycle, and stimmed training counts. About 30,000 roubles each on the flea; hand tremor for 45 s, so don't stim right before a fight.
    • Duo: hand off a heavy item. One player carries it (overweight, Strength) while the other stays light (Endurance). Every 3½ minutes, drop it for the other player to pick up. Free, and nobody backtracks. Swap in cover.
  • Carry-over slots are limited when you Prestige. Fill them in this order:
    1. Small, high-value items such as an LEDX skin transilluminator or a Physical Bitcoin. They sell for the most per slot and pay toward the next Prestige's rouble requirement.
    2. FiR items the next New Beginning asks for (for Prestige 2: 2 each of PMC, Scav, Boss and Trader figurines), or any other FiR hand-in that gates an early task on the route.
    3. Hard-to-find Collector items.
    Carried items keep their FiR status.
  • Prestiges that need Collector (5–6): Collector (Fence, level 42) opens after Shooter Born in Heaven (Mechanic), The Tarkov Shooter - Part 4 (Jaeger, Parts 1–4), Sew it Good - Part 2 (Ragman), Chemical - Part 3 (Skier) and Postman Pat - Part 2 (Prapor, then Therapist). Start the two bolt-action lines early; they're the slow ones. Lever-action rifles (Jaeger LL2) count as bolt-action and fire much faster, so use one. Otherwise the Remington Model 700 builds to the highest ergonomics of the bolt-actions.
  • Cultist Circle for task items. Sacrifice ₽400,000+ in base value (5 MP5s works; a Sacred Amulet adds 15%): 25% of the time you get a 6-hour timer that pays out items your active tasks and unfinished hideout modules need (hand-in items, not Gunsmith build parts). The fewer tasks you have active, the likelier it hits the one you want, even a LEDX. Don't hold back task accepts early to do this; use it once your active list is slim.
  • Labs keycards. Every New Beginning needs a Lab extract, so each Prestige needs a TerraGroup Labs access keycard (single use). Best early pick: Chemical - Part 4. It's the earliest task that pays out 2 cards, enough for the step 13 Lab run (Tour + The Ticket) and one return trip for New Beginning's Lab extract. Before you can buy them (flea market at level 30, about 95,000 roubles in PvP on Nov 3, 2026, or Therapist at LL4 for 166,500), these are the ways to get one; after that, buying is simpler:
    • Chemical - Part 4 (Skier, after Chemical - Part 3): 2 cards on completion; mark the chemical transport on Customs.
    • Camera, Action! (Mechanic, level 12, after Skier's Serious Allegations): 3 cards on completion for FiR SSD drive, USB Adapter, Portable Powerbank and Rechargeable battery.
    • Intelligence Center 2 craft: 3 cards from 1 Intelligence folder, 40 minutes; the UHF RFID Reader is a tool you get back. About a third of a folder's price per card, the cheapest repeatable source once IC2 is built.
    • Barters: Mechanic LL3 (Bulbex cable cutter + Pipe grip wrench), Ref LL3 (about 18 GP coins).
    Task data is from tarkov.dev; which trader group each task sits in under 1.1 may differ, so check the trader screen.
  • For Prestige 1–4 the hideout target is the same three modules, so building Intelligence Center 1, Nutrition Unit 2 and Rest Space 2 early is never wasted.

Before you start (money, menu steps, carrying items, hideout)
Money to set aside
  • 250,000 roubles for Streets (Tour)
  • 8,000 USD for Lighthouse (Tour). Peacekeeper pays in USD, so sell him loot you don't need; or buy USD from him with spare roubles
  • ~145,000 roubles for hideout modules below
  • 1 TerraGroup Labs access keycard minimum, for the step 13 Lab run. More is better: cards are single use, a failed run burns one, and later runs need more: Blue Fire, Boreas, The Unheard (2), the 30 minutes in The Lab if you accept Kerman, and every Prestige
How “Menu” steps work
  • Open Traders, click the trader, then click the task name.
  • If you're carrying the required items (FiR where the task says so), the hand-in button appears on the right side of the screen.
  • “Talk to” and “ask about” steps: select the trader's portrait, then press Visit at the top middle of the trader screen and pick the dialogue option.
  • Traders unlock through specific actions, not raid count. Each map step completes with a Survived or Run-Through extract on that map, or 3 visits to it:
    • Ragman (+ Streets): pay Therapist 250,000 roubles, then talk to Ragman.
    • Skier (+ Customs): finish the Interchange step, then tell Ragman what you found (Visit).
    • Mechanic (+ Factory): finish the Customs step, then hand Skier the 5 FiR building materials.
    • Prapor (+ Woods): finish the Factory step, then hand Mechanic 2 FiR weapons.
    • Peacekeeper (+ Shoreline): talk to Skier, then kill any 3 targets on Woods and finish the Woods step (the kills can be in a different raid from the extract).
    • Jaeger: hand Mechanic Jaeger's message from Introduction (found on Woods).
    • Ref: finish Skier's Easy Money - Part 1 (poster at the Customs Scav base).
  • Icons in the top-right corner of a trader's portrait tell you what's waiting. A green checkmark in a circle means a new task is waiting to be accepted. A plain checkmark (no circle) means a task's objectives are done and it's ready to turn in. The hand-in icon (a small grid of squares with an arrow pointing into it) means you have items in your stash that a task of theirs is asking for. A trader can show more than one at once. Check after every raid and every hand-in, since finishing tasks opens the next group.
Carrying task items
  • Items you bring to stash or hand in (neck chains, the DVL parts, keys): put them in your secure container when they fit, so a death doesn't cost them. Flares and MS2000 markers go in your special slots, where they're also safe; flares can be bound to a hotkey there.
  • Task items found in raid (the Farming package, Le Jean wine): they go to the Task items inventory on the Character tab, take no inventory space and can't be secured. Die before extracting and they're gone.
Hideout priorities
  • Intelligence Center 1 — gates The Ticket and adds 5% to task money. Needs Security 1, Vents 1, Generator 1, Workbench 1, plus a power cord, Intelligence folder, Topographic survey maps, Factory plan map and Working LCD. The parts are uncommon; collect them as you go (see Collect ahead).
  • Rest Space 2 — energy and health regeneration between raids. Needs Rest Space 1 and Generator 1.
  • Nutrition Unit 2 — energy, health and food crafts. Needs Nutrition Unit 1 (Generator 1) and Lavatory 2.
  • Cheap first steps: Vents 1 (25,000 roubles), Security 1 (20,000 + measuring tape), Workbench 1 (2 screw nuts, 2 bolts, Leatherman Multitool — buy from Mechanic once he unlocks), Generator 1 (100,000 + spark plug).

Collect ahead

Watch for these from step 1: 5 Printed circuit boards, 3 Rechargeable batteries and 2 Toolsets, all FiR. They're the Falling Skies hand-in at step 9 and the usual place the route stalls; the circuit boards and batteries are the hard part. Keep every FiR one you find, or craft them (Workbench 1: circuit board, toolset; Workbench 2: battery).

Rule: anything on this list that you see, take, whenever you have spare space. Picking it up before the task opens saves a dedicated raid later. Found in Raid (FiR) items must be extracted with on a PMC or Scav run (Scav loot counts as FiR), and hideout crafts count as FiR too. Store them; never sell them. Some tasks hand you the tool they need when you accept them (Fuel Crisis gives its 2 markers), so check your Messages after accepting a task, before buying. If a task comes with items, the trader sends them in a message there.

Story wish list

Every story hand-in on this route, in story order. FiR items must be looted or crafted (crafts always count as FiR); the rest can be bought.

Crafting power: crafts need the Generator running to start, except in the Lavatory, which needs no power. You can switch the Generator off after starting to save fuel, but the craft then runs more than 14× slower. Story crafts are the exception: keep power on until the timer ends.

Key list

Keys the route's tasks need: keep each until its task is done. Keys not on this list aren't needed by the route. Flea at level 25; no trader sells them. The Unknown, TerraGroup science office and Machinery keys spawn at their task spots.

Hideout wish list

Every item for your Prestige target's modules, totaled and A–Z for the in-game wish list; tick what you have. None need to be found in raid. Loot what you can, and buy the rest on the flea market once you reach the level on its tag. No rush to buy: hideout modules don't gate any task on this route except Workbench 1 (the Armored case craft at step 13, already on step 1's build list) and Intelligence Center 1, the first objective of The Ticket. Buy the rest when you're ready to build. The exception: if buying the last item or two finishes a module, do it early, since building it eats the parts you've been hoarding and frees stash space.

    Other story chapters

    Run in parallel

    Chapters start from more than their listed trigger. Picking up or reading an early clue or item from a chapter usually starts it too (Batya started from the patch alone), so you have something to bring up with the traders. Grab any you pass; the "may also start from" lines below are likely candidates, not confirmed. Each chapter is its own track: play its steps whenever the map comes up, alongside the main route or the cleanup loop. Steps tick off the same way; they don't count toward the main route's progress.

    Accidental Witness

    Starts from the note on Kozlov's door (room 110, two-story dorms on Customs), which is also its first required step. The wiki's listed start, the scribbled-on white car in the dorms courtyard, works too.

    Batya

    Starts when you pick up the Bogatyrs patch (step 5 has it at the Woods convoy). The wiki also lists mattress spots as starts: Customs Scav base 2nd floor, Reserve white queen radome, the bunker north of the Shoreline health resort, the big rock at the Woods USEC camp.

    Blue Fire

    Starts when you read an EMERCOM leaflet or the Note by Item #1156: Woods (green container at the Scav base/EMERCOM camp), Interchange (tents by Path to River and on the highway, or the EMERCOM unit on ULTRA's 1st floor), or The Labyrinth. May also start from the Unknown device fragment on Streets (LexOs closed section or the Chekannaya 13 marked room).

    Boreas

    Starts with a Paradigm Shipping poster, or by checking the distress signal on the Intelligence Center 3 radio. Adds the Icebreaker map. Posters spawn on Ground Zero walls: by the Emercom checkpoint, by the parking garage roller doors, and in the TerraGroup building hallway; Intelligence Center 3 isn't needed if you grab one. Then ask Mechanic about it in the menu: the Woods equipment repair (BO1) only appears after that, and the Lighthouse freight-yard directive only after the repair. Step C7 uses the repair as the start. Optional for Savior if you get the Ms. A. transcript instead (UN8).

    The Labyrinth

    Starts when you find The Labyrinth transit in the basement of the health resort west wing on Shoreline (Knossos LLC facility key). May also start from the Knossos LLC facility key itself (it spawns every raid at the health resort).

    The Unheard

    Starts when you read the Note with ramblings about the Purification: TerraGroup security post surveillance room or TerraGroup office on Streets, or Ground Zero (21+) office 4. May also start from its early Lab notes or the Factory transport log.

    They Are Already Here

    Cleanup loop · maps the route skips

    Steps C1–C10

    The numbered route unlocks the story and every map fastest, but these can be played at any point: first, between steps, or looped for variety. Their tasks count toward the trader tally whenever you tick them. Missed tasks with no later route step land here by map. Cleanup steps have no done box: rerun them as often as needed, and tick each task when you turn it in. These steps don't count toward the story progress.
