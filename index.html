Tarkov Story Route

The numbers are route steps, not a raid count. Repeat a step as often as you need, and only check it off when you move on. The steps carry the story; side tasks are in the map buttons on the bar.

Before you start (money, hideout, trader unlocks, menus, carrying items)
Money to set aside
  • 250,000 roubles for Streets (Tour)
  • 8,000 USD for Lighthouse (Tour). Peacekeeper pays in USD, so sell him loot you don't need; or buy USD from him with spare roubles
  • ~145,000 roubles for hideout modules below
  • 1 TerraGroup Labs access keycard minimum, for the step 13 Lab run (Therapist sells them at LL3; Mechanic and Ref barter them at LL3; Intelligence Center 2 crafts 3 at a time). More is better: cards are single use, a failed run burns one, and later runs need more: Blue Fire, Boreas, The Unheard (2), a 30-minute stretch in The Lab late in The Ticket, and every Prestige
Hideout priorities
  • Intelligence Center 1 — gates The Ticket and adds 5% to task money. Needs Security 1, Vents 1, Generator 1, Workbench 1, plus a power cord, Intelligence folder, Topographic survey maps, Factory plan map and Working LCD. The parts are uncommon; collect them as you go (see Collect ahead).
  • Rest Space 2 — energy and health regeneration between raids. Needs Rest Space 1 and Generator 1.
  • Nutrition Unit 2 — energy, health and food crafts. Needs Nutrition Unit 1 (Generator 1) and Lavatory 2.
  • Cheap first steps: Vents 1 (25,000 roubles), Security 1 (20,000 + measuring tape), Workbench 1 (2 screw nuts, 2 bolts, Leatherman Multitool — buy from Mechanic once he unlocks), Generator 1 (100,000 + spark plug).

How “Menu” steps work
  • Open Traders, click the trader, then click the task name.
  • If you're carrying the required items (FiR where the task says so), the hand-in button appears on the right side of the screen.
  • “Talk to” and “ask about” steps: select the trader's portrait, then press Visit at the top middle of the trader screen and pick the dialogue option.
Trader portrait icons
  • A green checkmark in a circle means a new task is waiting to be accepted. A plain checkmark (no circle) means a task's objectives are done and it's ready to turn in. The hand-in icon (a small grid of squares with an arrow pointing into it) means you have items in your stash that a task of theirs is asking for. A trader can show more than one at once. Check after every raid and every hand-in, since finishing tasks opens the next group.
Carrying task items
  • Items you bring to stash or hand in (neck chains, the DVL parts, keys): put them in your secure container when they fit, so a death doesn't cost them. Flares and MS2000 markers go in your special slots, where they're also safe; flares can be bound to a hotkey there.
  • Task items found in raid (the Farming package, Le Jean wine): they go to the Task items inventory on the Character tab, take no inventory space and can't be secured. Die before extracting and they're gone.

Collect ahead

Watch for these from step 1: 5 Printed circuit boards, 3 Rechargeable batteries and 2 Toolsets, all FiR. They're the Falling Skies hand-in at step 9 and the usual place the route stalls. Keep every FiR one you find, or craft them (Workbench 1: circuit board, toolset; Workbench 2: battery).

Rule: anything on this list that you see, take, whenever you have spare space. Picking it up before the task opens saves a dedicated raid later. Found in Raid (FiR) items must be extracted with on a PMC or Scav run (Scav loot counts as FiR), and hideout crafts count as FiR too. Store them; never sell them. Some tasks hand you the tool they need when you accept them (Fuel Crisis gives its 2 markers), so check your Messages after accepting a task, before buying. If a task comes with items, the trader sends them in a message there.

Story wish list

Every story hand-in on this route, in story order. FiR items must be looted or crafted (crafts always count as FiR); the rest can be bought.

Crafting power: crafts need the Generator running to start, except in the Lavatory, which needs no power. You can switch the Generator off after starting to save fuel, but the craft then runs more than 14× slower. Story crafts are the exception: keep power on until the timer ends.

Better: let the game do it. In the game's Settings, open the Auto-add to wish list drop-down, choose Hideout zone upgrade items, and save. Phase 1 · Tour opening and Prapor LL2

Tour runs the whole route and only completes at step 13: Shoreline Terminal (step 8), the Reserve and Lighthouse hand-ins (step 12), then The Lab (step 13).

Steps 1–7

Hardcore rules folded away.

Hardcore rules (zero to hero): you live off your own raids, your hideout and the traders.

  • After the tutorial raid, pay Therapist's 250,000 roubles for Streets, then DELETE all your other starting gear (don't sell it) as well as any currency: in the stash, hover an item and press your Discard hotkey. Keep only your secure container, and follow the route.
  • No flea market, no scaving. Traders only.
  • Keys only in your secure container, and only keys you've already extracted with. Identify a key only once it's made it out of raid to your stash. If you die and a key you hadn't extracted with survives in your container, delete it from your stash without ever identifying it. Everything else rides with you, so a death costs it.
  • Party up only with other Hardcore players, so nobody hands you gear or loot you couldn't have earned yourself.

Where a step says to put something in your secure container or buy it on the flea, Hardcore means finding and extracting with it, crafting it, or getting it from a trader. Moving story items to "Task items in stash" is still allowed.

    Other story chapters

    Run in parallel

    Chapters start from more than their listed trigger. Picking up or reading an early clue or item from a chapter usually starts it too (Batya starts from the patch alone), so you have something to bring up with the traders. Grab any you pass; the "may also start from" lines below are likely candidates, not confirmed. Each chapter is its own track: play its steps whenever the map comes up, alongside the main route or the cleanup loop. Steps check off the same way; they don't count toward the main route's progress.

    Accidental Witness

    Starts from the note on Kozlov's door (room 110, two-story dorms on Customs), which is also its first required step. The wiki's listed start, the scribbled-on white car in the dorms courtyard, works too.

    Batya

    Starts when you pick up the Bogatyrs patch (step 5 has it at the Woods convoy). The wiki also lists mattress spots as starts: Customs Scav base 2nd floor, Reserve white queen radome, the bunker north of the Shoreline health resort, the big rock at the Woods USEC camp.

    Blue Fire

    Starts when you read an EMERCOM leaflet or the Note by Item #1156: Woods (green container at the Scav base/EMERCOM camp), Interchange (tents by Path to River and on the highway, or the EMERCOM unit on ULTRA's 1st floor), or The Labyrinth. May also start from the Unknown device fragment on Streets (LexOs closed section or the Chekannaya 13 marked room).

    Boreas

    Starts with a Paradigm Shipping poster, or by checking the distress signal on the Intelligence Center 3 radio. Adds the Icebreaker map. Posters spawn on Ground Zero walls: by the Emercom checkpoint, by the parking garage roller doors, and in the TerraGroup building hallway; Intelligence Center 3 isn't needed if you grab one. Then ask Mechanic about it in the menu: the Woods equipment repair (BO1) only appears after that, and the Lighthouse freight-yard directive only after the repair. Step C7 uses the repair as the start. Optional for Savior if you get the Ms. A. transcript instead (UN8).

    The Labyrinth

    Starts when you find The Labyrinth transit in the basement of the health resort west wing on Shoreline (Knossos LLC facility key). May also start from the Knossos LLC facility key itself (it spawns every raid at the health resort).

    The Unheard

    Starts when you read the Note with ramblings about the Purification: TerraGroup security post surveillance room or TerraGroup office on Streets, or Ground Zero (21+) office 4. May also start from its early Lab notes or the Factory transport log.

    They Are Already Here

    Starts when you read the Note about the Eye of the World. Two spots need no key: the marked circle in the dilapidated village in the north of Woods (the note sits by the burning candles), or the house on the island on Shoreline (on the floor, in one of two spots). The Customs dorm and Reserve marked rooms have it too, but need a marked key.

    Prestige notes (skills, carry-over, Labs keycards, hideout)
    • Prestige 0 → 1 is mostly an XP run. Story is optional for it. This route is built from a fresh character for compatibility, but its real target is Prestige 2 and up.
    • Prestige 1 needs no story. PvP needs level 25, Ragman's New Beginning (level 25 to start: 50 Scavs, extract from The Lab, hand in any PMC, Scav, Boss and Trader figurine), the three hideout modules and 10M roubles. PvE needs only level 15 and 5M roubles.
    • This route's chapters map onto Prestige 2–4: Tour for Prestige 2, Falling Skies for 3, and obtaining the Ticket (end of step 13) for 4. The rest of The Ticket only matters from Prestige 6.
    • New Beginning (Prestige 2), from Ragman, unlocks once you have Prestige 1 and reach level 30 (20 in PvE); prestiging itself still needs level 30 (25 in PvE), per the table: eliminate 10 PMCs, extract from The Lab, hand in 2 FiR PMC figurines, 2 FiR Scav figurines, 2 FiR Boss figurines and 2 FiR Trader figurines.
    • Skills level from normal play. Strength levels while you move over your weight limit (the weight number turns from grey to yellow); Endurance levels while you move under it (grey number); the hideout Gym (Defective Wall 6, Illumination 2, Vents 2) also trains both. You don't need your own: open the player profile of a friend who has a Gym in the same game mode (PvP or PvE), choose view hideout, press Enter to walk in, go to their Gym and use the weight bench. Charisma comes from spending money with traders on items and services. Each Prestige also grants +1 Charisma.
      • Skill fatigue: in a raid, each skill slows sharply after about 5 points (red arrow next to the skill; 1.3.0.0 retuned this, so trust the arrow), and recovers to full speed after about 200 seconds (3⅓ minutes) without gains. So alternate: about 3½ minutes overweight (Strength), then about 3½ minutes under the limit (Endurance), and repeat for the whole raid.
      • Solo: 3-(b-TG) stim loop. Pack so you're just over the limit. The 3-(b-TG) gives Strength +10 for 4 minutes, which lifts your carry limit: stimmed you're under it (Endurance), and when it wears off you're over (Strength). One stim covers an 8-minute cycle, and stimmed training counts. About 30,000 roubles each on the flea; hand tremor for 45 s, so don't stim right before a fight.
      • Duo: hand off a heavy item. One player carries it (overweight, Strength) while the other stays light (Endurance). Every 3½ minutes, drop it for the other player to pick up. Free, and nobody backtracks. Swap in cover.
    • Carry-over slots are limited when you Prestige. Fill them in this order:
      1. Small, high-value items such as an LEDX skin transilluminator or a Physical Bitcoin. They sell for the most per slot and pay toward the next Prestige's rouble requirement.
      2. FiR items the next New Beginning asks for (for Prestige 2: 2 each of PMC, Scav, Boss and Trader figurines), or any other FiR hand-in that gates an early task on the route.
      3. Hard-to-find Collector items.
      Carried items keep their FiR status.
    • Prestiges that need Collector (5–6): Collector (Fence, level 42) opens after Shooter Born in Heaven (Mechanic), The Tarkov Shooter - Part 4 (Jaeger, Parts 1–4), Sew it Good - Part 2 (Ragman), Chemical - Part 3 (Skier) and Postman Pat - Part 2 (Prapor, then Therapist).     • Cultist Circle for task items. Sacrifice ₽400,000+ in base value (5 MP5s works; a Sacred Amulet adds 15%): 25% of the time you get a 6-hour timer that pays out items your active tasks and unfinished hideout modules need (hand-in items, not Gunsmith build parts). The fewer tasks you have active, the likelier it hits the one you want, even a LEDX. Don't hold back task accepts early to do this; use it once your active list is slim.
    • Labs keycards. Every New Beginning needs a Lab extract, so each Prestige needs a TerraGroup Labs access keycard (single use). Best early pick: Chemical - Part 4. It's the earliest task that pays out 2 cards, enough for the step 13 Lab run (Tour + The Ticket) and one return trip for New Beginning's Lab extract. Before you can buy them (Therapist at LL3, or the flea market at level 30: about 95,000 roubles in PvP on Nov 3, 2026), these are the ways to get one; after that, buying is simpler:
      • Chemical - Part 4 (Skier, after Chemical - Part 3): 2 cards on completion; mark the chemical transport on Customs.
      • Intelligence Center 2 craft: 3 cards from 1 Intelligence folder, 40 minutes; the UHF RFID Reader is a tool you get back. About a third of a folder's price per card, the cheapest repeatable source once IC2 is built.
      • Barters: Mechanic LL3 (Bulbex cable cutter + Pipe grip wrench), Ref LL3 (about 18 GP coins).

    FAQ

    Does the guide touch the game? No. It never opens the game's own files, memory or network traffic. With Game logs on, Chrome or Edge just reads the text log files the game writes automatically, read-only, the same logs community tools such as Tarkov Monitor read.

    Why a web page and not a program to install? So there's nothing to download, run or trust with your PC: no installer and no administrator rights, and it updates itself. You can still install it as an app from Chrome or Edge's menu, which is per-user and needs no administrator either. The whole guide is one open-source file you can read, or hand to an AI to check for you.

    Does anything need administrator rights? Only one case: Steam on a single drive, where a one-time setup file copies the logs somewhere Chrome may read them. Official-launcher installs and Steam with a second drive need nothing. Every route we tried before settling on this: README.

    Why do I still count items by hand? The logs don't record everything that happens. They note when a task starts, finishes or fails (and whether you're in a raid), but not items you pick up or hand in. So item counts stay manual until the game logs that task as completed: then it checks itself off and its items drop off your list.

    Why only Chrome or Edge on a PC? They're the browsers that let a web page read a folder you pick. Firefox, Safari and phones can't, but they can use the guide and link to a PC that has the logs connected.

    Keyboard shortcuts? Press / from anywhere on the page to search the loot list ("do I need this?"), and again to close it. Shift + / (the ? key) lists the rest: L loot list, C current step, A any-map tasks, I item list, W wish lists, G gunsmith, 1–9 and 0 for the maps, Esc to close. The number pad, or Shift + 1–0, switches maps even while you type in the loot search; Enter there means done typing so the other keys work again. They only work while the guide's window has focus and you're not typing, so they never reach the game: with Tarkov in windowed or borderless mode, alt-tab to the guide, press a key, alt-tab back.

    What leaves my PC? Nothing from the logs. If you join a party or link a device, party sync shares the name you type (any name you like), checked tasks and steps, loot needs and in raid · map. Account and profile numbers stay on your device. Streamer mode (in the party row of the loot list) stops sharing in raid · map entirely and keeps party codes, invite links and QR codes covered on screen until you click or tap them. Could a stranger land in your party? Party codes are 8 characters from 32 letters and digits, about 1.1 trillion combinations, so not by chance: joining takes the code or your invite link, the relay turns away repeated guessing, and an empty party's code expires. To go quiet for a while, Leave, then rejoin from Recent parties with one tap.
